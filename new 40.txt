1.	Please print the approval letter(s) and bring it with you to Canada along with your original visa-endorsed passport(s). You will need to show it to obtain your work permit.
2.	After discussing and making arrangements with your employer, you can travel to Canada with your approval letter(s) and visa-endorsed passport(s) to obtain your work permit.
3.	It is recommended that if travelling to Canada by air, travellers enter through major international airports (e.g. Toronto Pearson or Vancouver International) during business hours to ensure that officers are present for immigration processing. If travelling by land, Land Crossing hours can be confirmed by consulting the listing of border crossings under “ Browse by province/territory .”
4.	Upon your entry to Canada, you will be asked by a CBSA officer for your purpose in Canada. You will show your documentation and advise you need to obtain your work permit. You will be directed to a secondary office.
5.	Once there, an officer will review your file and issue the permit(s) to you. Please make sure to check your permit(s) before leaving that office, to ensure its accuracy.
Specifically, your work permit should include the following:
o	Occupation: Technical Architect
o	Employer: Coforge DPA Private Limited
o	Location: Ontario and British Colombia 
o	Remarks: “Authorized to work at various company and client locations
across Canada as designated by the employer”
6.	Upon receipt of your permit(s), and confirmation that it is accurate, you can be on your way.
7.	Kindly provide us a copy of your permit(s) upon your receipt so that we may track its expiry on your behalf for renewal purposes.
